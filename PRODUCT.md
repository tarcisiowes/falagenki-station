# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The primary user is an adult Brazilian Portuguese speaker studying Japanese independently on mobile and desktop. They use the app during focused lessons, short review sessions, listening practice, and progress checks. The main job is to turn structured source material into practice that is understandable, actionable, and easy to revisit.

## Product Purpose

falaGENKI no Eki is a personal, non-commercial Japanese study workspace. It organizes JLPT, Irodori, and Genki material into explanations, examples, audio, exercises, and local FSRS review. Success means that every study item communicates why it exists, what the learner should do, what a correct answer looks like, and how the item returns for review.

## Brand Personality

Calm, didactic, and practical. The interface should feel serious without becoming austere, supportive without becoming childish, and structured without becoming dense or bureaucratic.

## Anti-references

Avoid controls that have no content or visible effect, media presented without a study purpose, decorative gamification, childish classroom styling, excessive card nesting, and screens that expose implementation details instead of guiding the next learning action.

## Design Principles

1. Every control earns its place through a clear state change, useful content, or feedback.
2. Turn passive material into active, reviewable practice while preserving source fidelity.
3. Explain the learning purpose before exposing secondary options or details.
4. Keep the primary study action obvious and reveal support progressively.
5. Design for comfortable reading, listening, and touch on mobile first, without weakening desktop use.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA fundamentals for contrast, keyboard focus, semantic controls, and status feedback. Keep touch targets comfortable on Android and iOS, support reduced motion, never rely on color alone, and preserve readable Japanese typography with optional furigana and Brazilian Portuguese support.
